Getting started

Is there a web version?

No. Gasolinahan is a mobile app for iPhone and Android, and this website is only for information and support. Install the app from your phone’s app store.

Is it free?

Yes. Finding prices, reporting them, logging fill-ups and tracking trips are all free.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and we’ll email you a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn’t arrive within a few minutes.

Prices & reports

Why is a price wrong?

Prices come from other drivers and stations can change them at any time. Check how old the price is, and when you’re at the station, report the current price to fix it for everyone.

How do I report prices?

Open the station, tap report, and either scan the price board with your camera or type the prices in. Check the numbers, then send. If you’re offline, the report is saved and sent when you’re back online.

The scanner read the board wrong.

Try again with the whole board in the frame, held steady, without glare. You can always correct any number before sending.

How do points work?

You earn points for price reports and fill-ups and move up from Bronze to Silver, Gold and Platinum. Points have no cash value and expire after 12 months. See the terms.

Trips & location

Why does it ask for “Always” location?

Only for automatic trip tracking, which is optional. If you just want prices, choose “While using the app”. Trips are stored on your phone and never uploaded.

Trips aren’t recording automatically.

Allow location “Always” (iPhone) or “Allow all the time” (Android), allow Motion & fitness or Physical activity, and don’t force-close the app. Some Android phones stop background apps to save battery; turn off battery optimization for Gasolinahan. The app has a step-by-step guide under automatic tracking.

Will tracking drain my battery?

Tracking uses low-power location until you’re driving, and short trips under half a kilometer are ignored. You can turn tracking off at any time.

Account & privacy

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. Or see Delete your account to ask us by email.

Who can see my reports?

Everyone sees the station prices, but not who reported them. Your report history, fill-ups and points are visible only to you. More in the privacy policy.
