Delete your account

You can delete your Gasolinahan account and its data at any time, in the app or by asking us.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Gasolinahan and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted right away, and you’re signed out.

Ask us by email

If you no longer have the app or can’t sign in, email us from the address you use to sign in, so we can confirm the account is yours.

Email a deletion request

Or write to tibudan.rafael@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Gasolinahan account”. If we can’t confirm the account is yours, we’ll ask you to verify it before deleting anything.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and password
  • Your profile photo
  • Your fill-up log, including where each fill-up was logged
  • Your points, tier and points history
  • Your price reports
  • Saved stations

When you delete from inside the app, the data stored on that phone is also removed: recorded trips and routes, fill-up photos, and reports waiting to sync. If you ask us by email, this on-device data stays on your phone until you uninstall the app.

What we keep

Station prices you helped update stay on the shared map, because other drivers rely on them. They carry no name, email or user ID, so they can’t be traced back to you once your account is deleted.

We don’t keep any other personal data after deletion, except where the law requires us to.

How long it takes

In-app deletion happens immediately. We act on email requests within 30 days and confirm by email when it’s done. Copies in our providers’ backup systems are removed on their normal schedules after that.
