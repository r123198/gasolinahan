Your account

Keep your password private and the details on your account accurate. You’re responsible for what happens under your account. You can delete it at any time; see Delete your account.

Prices are crowdsourced

Prices in Gasolinahan come from other users and may be wrong or out of date. Stations can change prices at any time. Always check the price at the pump before you pay.

We show how old each price is, but we don’t check every report and can’t guarantee that any price is accurate, complete or current.

Reporting fairly

When you use Gasolinahan, you agree not to:

  • submit prices you know are false, or report stations you haven’t seen;
  • game the points program, for example with fake reports, multiple accounts or automation;
  • interfere with the app or its servers, or try to access other people’s data;
  • use the app while driving in a way that puts you or others at risk.

We may remove reports, take back points, or suspend accounts that break these rules.

Points

  • Points recognize your contributions. They have no cash value and can’t be sold, transferred or exchanged for money.
  • Points expire 12 months after you earn them.
  • We may change how points are earned, what they unlock, and the tiers, or end the program. If we make a major change, we’ll tell you in the app.
  • Points awarded by mistake or through abuse may be taken back.
  • Deleting your account deletes your points.

Trip tracking & driving

Trip distances and fuel figures are estimates based on your phone’s sensors and the data you enter. Don’t rely on them for official, tax or legal records. Never use your phone in a way that distracts you from driving; report prices only when you’re safely stopped.
