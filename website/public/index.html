How it works

Three stops, no detours.

  1. Check the map

    See stations around you with their latest prices and how recently they were updated.

  2. Fill up & report

    At the pump, scan the price board or type the prices. It takes a few seconds and helps every driver nearby.

  3. Watch your budget

    Log the fill-up and Gasolinahan works out your spend, kilometers and cost per kilometer for you.

Privacy

Your drives are your business.

We built Gasolinahan to need as little of your data as possible. Read the full privacy policy.

Stays on your phone

  • Your trips and routes. Automatic tracking is optional and never uploaded.
  • Price board and odometer photos. Read on your phone; the photo isn’t uploaded.
  • Fill-up photos. Kept in the app on your device.

We never

  • Show ads or include advertising SDKs.
  • Use analytics trackers to follow what you do.
  • Sell or share your data for advertising. Prices you report appear without your name.

Questions

Before you ask.

Is Gasolinahan free?

Yes. Finding prices, reporting them, logging fill-ups and tracking trips are all free.

Where do the prices come from?

From other drivers. Each report shows how old it is. Prices can change at any time, so treat them as a guide and check the pump before you pay.

Why does it ask for “Always” location?

Only for automatic trip tracking, which is optional. If you just want prices, allow location while using the app. Your trips stay on your phone.
