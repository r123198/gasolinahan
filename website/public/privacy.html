The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account details, the prices you report, and the fill-ups you log.
  • Trips, routes and photos stay on your phone. Price boards and odometers are read on your phone; the photos aren’t uploaded.
  • No ads, no analytics trackers, and we don’t sell or share your data for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time from the app or from this website.

Who we are

Gasolinahan is a mobile app published by Maalam (“we”, “us”). We are the personal information controller for the data described here. You can reach us at tibudan.rafael@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Gasolinahan apps for iPhone and Android and this website. The website itself has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics.

What we collect and why

Your account

  • Name, email address and password, to create your account and sign you in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Profile photo (optional), if you add one. It’s stored in Google Firebase Storage.
  • Points, tier and report count, to run the points program.
  • Saved stations, if you mark stations as favorites.

Price reports

When you report prices, we store the station, the prices and fuel products, the station’s location, the time, and your user ID. Your user ID lets you see your report history, lets us award points, and helps us spot false reports. Each report also updates the station’s current prices.

Fill-up log

Fill-ups you log (station, fuel, amount spent, liters, price per liter, odometer reading, notes, date, and the location where you logged it) are saved on your phone and synced to your account, so they follow you to a new phone. We use them only to show you your own spending and efficiency.

Location for nearby stations

While you’re using the app, your location is used to center the map, find nearby stations, and suggest which station you’re at when you report or log a fill-up. We don’t store a history of your location on our servers. Location is saved only as part of a price report or fill-up that you choose to submit.

What we don’t collect

We don’t use advertising IDs, ad networks, analytics SDKs or crash-reporting services. We don’t collect your contacts, messages or payment details.

What stays on your phone

  • Trips. If you turn on automatic trip tracking, your trips and routes are recorded and stored only on your phone. They aren’t uploaded to us.
  • Price board and odometer photos. When you scan a price board or odometer, the text is read on your phone using Google ML Kit’s on-device text recognition. Only the numbers you confirm are used. The photo itself isn’t uploaded.
  • Fill-up photos you attach stay in the app on your phone.
  • Reports made offline wait on your phone until you’re back online, then sync.

Uninstalling the app removes this on-device data. Deleting your account from inside the app removes it too.

Who we share data with

We don’t sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in (Authentication), database (Cloud Firestore) and file storage (Cloud Storage). Google processes this data on our behalf. See Firebase’s privacy information.
  • Google ML Kit: on-device text recognition. Images are processed on your phone. ML Kit may send Google limited information about the device and how the feature performs, but not your images. See ML Kit terms.
  • OpenStreetMap services. Map images come from the OpenStreetMap Foundation’s tile servers. To name your area and find mapped stations near you, the app sends coordinates to the Nominatim service (OpenStreetMap Foundation) and the public Overpass API (overpass-api.de). These services receive your device’s IP address and the map area or coordinates requested, under their own policies: OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app through the App Store and Google Play under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety, or to investigate fraud or abuse of the service.

What other users can see

Station prices, and when they were last updated, are visible to every Gasolinahan user. Other users don’t see your name, email or user ID next to a price. Your profile, fill-up log, points and report history are visible only to you.

How long we keep data

  • Account data, fill-ups, points history and reports are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Station prices are kept as part of the shared price map. They contain no personal information once your report record is deleted.
  • Points expire 12 months after you earn them, as described in the terms of use.
  • Copies in our providers’ backup systems are removed on their normal schedules after deletion.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account and confirm with your password. This deletes your account, fill-up log, points history, price reports and profile photo from our servers, and the trips and photos stored on that phone. You can also ask us to delete it by email.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), and similar laws where you live, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal data is processed;
  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data. You can change your name and photo in the app;
  • object to processing, and withdraw consent, for example by turning off a permission;
  • have your data erased or blocked;
  • data portability, receiving your data in a commonly used format;
  • be compensated for damages caused by inaccurate or unlawfully processed data;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To use any of these rights, email tibudan.rafael@gmail.com from the address on your account. We’ll reply within 30 days.

Security

Data travels to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and is stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts data at rest. Database access rules let each account read and change only its own private data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don’t use anywhere else. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we’ll notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

Where data is stored

Google Firebase may store and process data on servers outside the Philippines, including in the United States. Google applies contractual and technical safeguards to protect it.

Children

Gasolinahan is made for drivers and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we’ll also tell you in the app before they take effect.
